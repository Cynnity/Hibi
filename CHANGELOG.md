# Hibi patch notes

Everything that's changed in Hibi, newest first. Versions 0.1 to 1.0 all happened in one late night (October 20–21, 2026).

**How the version numbers work:**
- **Big number (2.0, 3.0):** a huge update that changes how Hibi feels.
- **Middle number (2.3 → 2.4):** new features.
- **Last number (2.4 → 2.4.1):** small fixes and tweaks.

## 4.4 — "Split the Stash" (Oct 27)
- **Your monthly savings can go to up to 3 goals now.** In Budget › Plan › Savings there's a new "Split between goals" section. Pick up to 3 Savings goals and the percent each one gets (it has to add up to 100).
- **One tap moves it all.** On the Budget overview, the Savings card lists each goal with its share, and "Move into your goals" puts the right amount into each one in Savings.
- Picking a single goal for one month still works and overrides the split for that month. "Use one goal" goes back to the old way.
- (The Japan fund link from 4.3 is still there in Japan › Money.)

## 4.3.1 — "Three's Company" (Oct 27)
- **Link as many Savings goals as you want to your Japan trip fund.** Trip fund now has a "Link another" picker, so you can tie in 3 or more goals (flights, spending money, souvenirs, whatever you've set up in Savings).
- Each linked goal has an Unlink button. Your budget adds up all the linked goals.
- When you Add or Take out in Japan with more than one goal linked, you pick which goal it goes to.

## 4.3 — "One Pot" (Oct 27)
- **Your Japan fund now links to Savings.** In Japan › Money there's a new 💰 Trip fund card. Link one of your Savings goals (or make a new "Japan trip" goal in one tap) and the two stay in sync.
- **Add and Take out right from Japan.** Type an amount and tap Add or Take out. It goes into your Savings goal too, with the same goal-reached celebration, and it won't let you take out more than is saved.
- **Your budget follows your savings.** Turn on "Use it as my budget" and the left-to-spend number in Japan is what's saved minus what you've booked and spent. Put more in Savings and it updates by itself.
- Turn it off any time to go back to the budget number you typed.

## 4.2.1 — "Receipts and Recents" (Oct 27)
- **New Recent card on Budget Overview.** It shows your latest activity in one list: purchases you added, money in, subscriptions and bills you marked paid, and card payments. Newest first, with the date and category on each line.
- Tap a purchase to edit it, or tap See all to open the full Spending list.

## 3.8 — "Snap, Read, Done" (Oct 27)
- **Scan a receipt.** In Budget, tap **📷 Scan a receipt** (Spending tab, or Overview under Add spending). Take a photo or pick one from your library.
- **It reads the receipt right on your phone.** Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Hibi straightens and cleans up the photo, reads the text, and fills in the **store, date, total, category and which card** you paid with.
- **You always check it before it's saved.** A confirm card shows the photo and every field. If the total looks uncertain it says so, and it offers the other amounts it saw as one-tap choices.
- **Smart extras:** it double-checks the total against subtotal + tax (and tip on restaurant receipts), guesses gas stations from the gallons line, and can save the items into the purchase's note.
- **Cards remember their last 4 digits.** Add them in Budget › Accounts › Card rewards. When a receipt shows "ending 1942", Hibi picks that card for you. The first time, it offers to remember the digits.
- **Keep the photo.** Receipts you keep stay on your phone. Tap a purchase, then 🧾 Receipt, to see it again.
- **First scan downloads the reader once (about 13 MB).** After that it works offline, and app updates don't download it again.
- Receipt photos are not in the Dropbox backup yet.

## 3.7.1 — "Month by Month Savings" (Oct 27)
- **Savings are now set per month.** In Plan, the Savings card belongs to the month you're looking at (this month, next month, after that). Changing it there no longer changes any other month. You can pick the amount or percent and a savings goal for just that month.
- **Your usual savings moved to Settings › Budget.** That is what every month uses until you set one in Plan.
- Plan has "Make this my usual" to copy a month's savings to all months, and "Back to my usual" to undo a month's own setting.

## 3.7 — "Worth It" (Oct 26)
- **Net worth.** Budget › Accounts opens with a Net worth card: what you own minus what you owe, how much it has grown since you started, a little trend line, and a bar splitting spending, savings, investing and owed.
- **Savings accounts.** Each account can be Checking or cash, Savings or Investing, so your savings have their own slice.
- **Credit card balances and limits.** Each card shows what you owe, your credit limit, how much of it you have used and how much is available. Under about 30% is the healthy zone.
- **Which card?** Every purchase now has a "Which card" picker, and what you owe goes up with it. Imports can pick a card for the whole file, or read a Card column.
- **Card payments.** Log a payment to a card and what you owe goes down. It also comes off your tracked account, but it isn't counted as spending.
- Purchases on a card no longer lower your tracked account until you pay the card, which matches how credit works.

## 3.6.1 — "One Wallet, Three Cards" (Oct 26)

- **Points or cash back.** Each card has "each point is worth", so cash back counts as 1 and points or miles can be about 0.01. Type your points, and Hibi shows the dollars.

## 3.6 — "Points Well Spent" (Oct 26)
- **Card rewards.** Accounts now has a Card rewards box. Type what your points or cash back are worth, and it counts down as you use them.
- **Pay with rewards.** Any purchase has a new "Paid with card rewards" amount. That part comes off your rewards instead of your safe to spend, so it acts a bit like extra money without being income. Partial amounts work.

## 3.5 — "Money Back" (Oct 26)
- **Hibi spots refunds.** When money comes in that matches something you bought (same amount or part of it, similar name, within 60 days), it shows up in Review as "Looks like a refund for…". Swipe right to confirm, left if it's something else.
- **Confirmed refunds cancel the spending.** That purchase comes back off its category and month, so safe to spend goes back up. The refund isn't counted as income. Say no and it stays as normal money in.
- **Imports pair them up.** Even with "Also import money coming in" off, a credit that matches a purchase is brought in for you to confirm. Everything else is skipped like before.
- Spending shows "$21.04 refunded" on the purchase and "Refund of Apple" on the money in.

## 3.4.2 — "Counted Once" (Oct 26)
- **Fixed income being counted twice.** If you typed what you expect to make and also imported your paychecks as money coming in, Budget added both together, so safe to spend looked far too high. Imported money in now replaces your estimate instead of stacking on top of it. Once more arrives than you expected, the real total is used.
- Money you add by hand (a gift, something you sold) still adds on top, as before.

## 3.3 — "Safe to Spend" (Oct 26)
- **A whole Budget mode** (💸), built to feel like Rocket Money with your own twists. Five tabs: Overview, Spending, Bills, Plan and Settings.
  - **Safe to spend, front and center.** Hibi takes your paycheck, sets aside bills and savings first, then splits what's left across the days until your next payday. The big number is what you can spend today, with a daily amount and a "you're under or over your usual pace" line.
  - **Runs on your paydays.** Budget periods follow your Work pay periods (Oct 23 to Nov 5, then Nov 6 to Nov 19, and so on), and the income comes from your real Work paychecks. You can switch to calendar months, or type your pay by hand, in Settings.
  - **Auto-sorting.** Type or import a purchase and Hibi files it: gas stations and fuel into Gas, SunPass, tolls and parking into Car & tolls, movies and games into Entertainment, pharmacy and gym into Health, plus Groceries, Eating out, Shopping, Travel, School and more. It reads messy bank text like `SHELL OIL 57444 OKEECHOBEE FL` and ignores store numbers.
  - **Swipe review.** When Hibi isn't sure (a Walmart run could be groceries or shopping), it goes into a card deck on Spending › Review. Swipe right if it's right, swipe left to pick a different category. Hibi remembers your choice for that merchant and sorts the other waiting ones too.
  - **Bills come out first.** Your subscriptions from Savings and your own bills (rent, phone, insurance) are set aside before your safe-to-spend number is worked out, so paying them never pushes you over. Tick them off as paid, or let Hibi spot the payment in your spending.
  - **Plan this period or the next ones.** Set a budget for each category, change income or savings for just one period, copy a plan forward, save it as your default, or tap Suggest to fill it in from your recent spending.
  - **Savings built in.** Set aside a dollar amount or a percent of each paycheck, point it at a Savings goal, and move it into the goal with one tap.
  - **Make it yours.** Add, rename, recolor, reorder and delete categories, and edit the words that sort into each one. A word ending in ? sorts the purchase but asks you to double-check it. Optional rollover of leftovers into the next period.
  - **Import from your bank.** Choose a CSV file or paste rows. Hibi finds the columns, skips payments and transfers, doesn't add duplicates, and shows a preview first. You can also export everything as a CSV.
  - A **Safe to spend** widget for Home (small or wide), five new badges, and a Budget shortcut when you press and hold the app icon. `?mode=budget` opens it directly.
- **Fix:** Home widgets from later files, like Subscriptions, no longer disappear after you close and reopen the app.
- **Fix:** the old `#budget` link now opens this Budget mode. The Japan trip budget is `#japanbudget`.

## 3.2 — "Lecture Hall" (Oct 23)
- **Notebooks with Apple Pencil**, in School › Study › Notebooks. Made to feel like GoodNotes and Notability:
  - **Pen** with pressure, so lines get thicker as you press harder. Pick from 8 colors (or any color) and 3 sizes.
  - **Highlighter** that goes under your writing, in 5 colors.
  - **Eraser** that removes whole strokes.
  - **Lasso** to circle writing, then drag it somewhere else, recolor it or delete it.
  - **Hold at the end of a line** to snap it perfectly straight.
  - **Undo and redo** (⌘Z works with a keyboard too).
  - **Your palm is ignored.** Only Apple Pencil writes, and your finger scrolls. Turn on "Draw with finger" in ⋯ if you want.
  - **Paper:** blank, lined, grid, dots or **Cornell** (cues, notes and summary), on white, cream or dark paper. Black ink turns white on dark paper so you can still read it.
  - **Photo pages:** add a photo or screenshot of a slide as a page and write right on top of it.
  - Pages scroll like a real notebook. Add as many as you want.
  - **Export** a whole notebook as a PDF, or one page as an image.
  - Notebook covers come in 8 colors and can belong to a class.
- **Record lectures** right inside a notebook with 🎙️:
  - Pause and resume. A red pill on every screen shows you're still recording, even after you leave the notebook.
  - **Live transcript:** your iPad's built-in speech recognition writes down what it hears while it records. If it isn't available, the audio still records.
  - **Your notes sync with the audio:** while a recording plays, writing you hadn't done yet fades out and fills back in as the lecture plays. Tap 🔊, then anything you wrote, to jump to that moment in the lecture.
  - The player has skip back and forward, speed (0.75× to 2×) and a full transcript you can tap to jump around.
  - Share the **audio** (.m4a), the **transcript** (.txt) or copy the transcript. Send the transcript or the PDF to Claude and ask for clean notes or a flashcard file.
  - Audio saves every few seconds, so even if something goes wrong you keep what was recorded.
  - Keep Hibi open and the screen on while recording. Hibi keeps the screen awake for you, but iPhone and iPad stop web apps from recording once you leave them.
- **Backed up:** notebook pages, recordings and transcripts go to Dropbox › Apps › your Hibi folder (`notebooks/` and `recordings/`), and come back when you restore.
- A **Notebook widget** for Home opens your latest notebook, and shows the timer while you're recording.
- New School badges: First Page, Note Machine, Lecture Mode and Front Row.

## 3.1 — "Study Arc" (Oct 23)
- **Flashcards, Quizlet style.** School's Notes tab is now **Study**, with Flashcards and Notes inside it.
- **Make decks your way:**
  - Import a text file, or paste cards in. Use one card per line, like `Ohm's law :: V = I × R`. Dashes, colons and tabs work too, so Quizlet exports import as they are.
  - A line starting with `#` starts a new deck, so one file can hold several decks.
  - Tap **🃏 Make flashcards** on any note to turn it into a deck.
  - Add, edit, star and delete cards by hand (swipe left to delete), link a deck to a class, and export a deck back out as a text file.
- **Study modes:**
  - **🃏 Flashcards:** tap to flip, then swipe right if you know it or left if you're still learning. You can undo, and at the end you can study just the ones you missed.
  - **🧠 Learn:** rounds of 7 cards. You get multiple choice first, then you type the answer. You're done when every card is mastered.
  - **📝 Test:** pick how many questions and which kinds (multiple choice, true or false, written). You get a score, every answer you missed, and a button to Learn just those.
  - **⚡ Match:** tap each term and its definition against the clock. Wrong pairs cost a second, and Hibi keeps your best time for each deck.
  - **🔁 Review:** cards come back right before you'd forget them (spaced repetition). "Know it" pushes a card further out; missing it brings it back today.
- **Options:** shuffle, starred cards only, and show the term or the definition first.
- **Typed answers are forgiving.** Small typos, capitals, accents, extra spaces and anything in brackets don't count against you. "v = i x r" matches "V = I × R". If Hibi still marks you wrong when you were right, tap **I was right**.
- A **Flashcards widget** for Home shows how many cards are due, with a Review button.
- New School badges: Deck Builder, Card Shark, Flashcard Grind, Learned It, Perfect Score and Speed Matcher. Flashcards studied also show up in your weekly recap.

## 3.0 — "Season 3: Everything, Everywhere" (Oct 23)
The biggest update yet: two new modes, a whole new way to customize Hibi, and gestures that make it feel like a real iPhone app.

- **New mode: 🌿 Health.**
  - **Water:** a daily goal in fl oz (64 to start), with +8 oz, +16 oz and one-tap "+ your bottle" buttons. Undo the last drink, see which days you hit your goal, and keep a streak going.
  - **Habits:** make your own daily habits, like Stretch or Read 10 pages, each with an emoji and the days it's on (every day, weekdays, weekends, or pick days). Check them off for 🔥 streaks and your best streak. There's a list of ideas to start from.
  - **Sleep:** log when you went to bed and woke up, plus how it felt. You get hours slept, your weekly and 30-day average, your usual bedtime and a chart. Set your own sleep goal.
  - New Home widgets for water, habits and sleep. Water and habits were added to your Home for you.
  - New badges for Health, and water, habits and sleep now show up in your weekly recap.
- **New mode: 🛒 Lists.**
  - **Shopping list:** type something like "2 dozen eggs" and Hibi sorts it into store sections like Produce, Dairy and Household. Check things off as you shop, then clear them. Things you've bought before show up under Buy again.
  - **Wish list:** add things you want with a link, a price and a target price. Every time you log the price, Hibi keeps the history and shows drops, like "▼ $60 since Oct 3", plus a little price chart. It tells you when something hits your target. "Save for it" turns it into a Savings goal.
  - About price tracking: Hibi can't check store prices by itself, because stores block apps from reading their pages. So you tap Check price and log what you see.
  - Widgets for both lists, and badges too.
- **Swipe and drag.**
  - **Swipe right** to check something off and **swipe left** to delete or edit it. This works on habits, the shopping list, homework, Japan to-dos and packing, your Ideas list, weigh-ins and sleep. Deleting with a swipe gives you an **Undo** button.
  - **Drag ⠿ to reorder** instead of using arrows. This works for modes, Home widgets, workout templates, your Up next games, habits and your wish list.
- **Haptic taps.** You get a light tap when you check things off, swipe or drag. On iPhone this needs iOS 18 or later, and you can turn it off under Look. When rest is over in the Gym, Android phones vibrate too. iPhone doesn't let websites vibrate on their own, so the iPhone gets the beep.
- **Make Hibi yours (Settings › Look).**
  - **Accent color:** 20 colors or pick any color you want, on top of any theme.
  - **Card style:** Pop (the classic look), Soft, Outline or Glass.
  - **Mode icons:** pick any emoji for each mode.
  - **App icon:** 10 colors of the Hibi flower. iPhone only sets a web app's icon when you add it to the Home Screen, so Hibi walks you through adding it again with your backup. That way nothing gets lost.
  - **5 new themes:** Lavender, Peach, Sky, Mocha and Berry.
- **Easier to find things.**
  - Tap **🔎** at the start of the mode bar to jump to any screen, or search for one, like "water", "flights" or "backup".
  - Settings is now split into sections: this mode's settings, Look, Modes, Passcode, Backup and About.
- **Links straight to a mode:** add ?mode=gym, #savings, #water or #shopping to Hibi's address. On Android, press and hold the icon for shortcuts.
  - On iPhone, a link opens in Safari, which keeps separate data from your Home Screen app, so these are mostly for Android.
- **Launch screen:** adding Hibi to the Home Screen now also sets a launch screen that matches your icon.
- **Body weight:** you can now log a weight for any date, and edit or delete old weigh-ins to fix mistakes (tap Edit, or swipe left).

## 2.5 — "Jet lag protocol" (Oct 22)
- **Airport time zones.** Hibi now knows the time zone for most airports, including every US hub and all of Japan's. Each flight shows:
  - which time zone its times are in, like "Departs, Eastern time" or "Arrives, Japan time"
  - how long you're actually in the air
  - what time you land in Eastern time, like "That's Tue, Jan 12, 1:00 AM Eastern time"
  - your total travel time for each direction
- **Fixed:** with a round trip of 4 flights, the flight home got split up and put in the wrong order. Flying back from Tokyo, you "land" earlier on the clock than you left, which confused Hibi. It now sorts flights by their real times, so "Flying home: HND → DFW → MCO" stays together with the right layover.
- **One price for one booking.** Booked a round trip as one ticket? Give all the flights the same confirmation code. Your budget counts the price once instead of once per flight, and the flight with the price shows "Price for all 4 flights".
- **Unknown airport?** Hibi tells you and lets you pick the time zone in the flight form.
- **Calendar export** now puts flights and layovers at their exact real times.
- **"Add a connecting flight"** now also fills in the airline, confirmation code and the time you landed.

## 2.4.1 — "Plot armor" (Oct 21)

## 2.4 — "Subscription season" (Oct 21)
- **New Subscriptions tab in Savings 🔁.** Add each subscription by hand with its price, how often it charges (monthly, yearly, weekly, every 3 or 6 months) and its next charge date.
  - **Totals:** what you pay a month and a year, plus your biggest costs.
  - **Quick picks** for common ones like Netflix, Spotify, Crunchyroll, Game Pass and iCloud+. They fill in the name and emoji so you just add the price.
